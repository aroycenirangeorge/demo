EXECUTION METHODOLOGY

Seven-Stage Turnkey Delivery Process

A structured, transparent workflow designed to eliminate guesswork, control budgets, and ensure timely handover across every project scale.

01

Requirement Study

We understand the site, project purpose, functional requirements, preferred style, timeline, and investment range.

02

Site Assessment

We verify dimensions, existing conditions, access, structural feasibility, and available utilities before planning begins.

03

Concept & Planning

Layouts, design intent, 2D plans, 3D views, and coordinated drawings are developed around the approved direction.

04

BOQ, Costing & Approval

A clear scope, Bill of Quantities (BOQ), itemized costing, material selections, payment milestones, and project schedule are finalized.

05

Execution

Approved plans are executed through coordinated civil, MEP, joinery, and finishing work under site supervision.

06

Quality Inspection

Work is inspected at defined stages for specification, alignment, service performance, safety, and finish quality.

07

Handover

Snag points are closed, final cleaning is completed, and the finished project is handed over after client review.
